Free calculators for money, taxes, and home projects

Two small sites with calculators that run in your browser, plus a recipe add-on. No sign-up, no ads, and the sources for every tax number are cited on the page.

Simple Money Sheets

Personal finance and self-employed tax calculators.

  • Debt payoff calculator (snowball vs avalanche)
  • Budget calculator
  • Quarterly estimated tax calculator
  • Estimated tax penalty calculator (Form 2210)

DIY Project Calculators

How much to buy for home and yard projects.

  • Concrete calculator (bags or yards)
  • Gravel calculator
  • Mulch calculator
  • Paint calculator

Recipe Card

A free browser add-on that shows just the recipe: ingredients and steps, servings scaling, metric/US units, cook mode, and print. Try the demo.

freelance-tax-estimator

The open-source JavaScript engine behind the self-employed tax calculators, with a command-line version.
