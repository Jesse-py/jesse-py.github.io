About

Simple Tool Shop is run by Jesse McCormick in Casper, Wyoming. It started as two calculator sites, Simple Money Sheets and DIY Project Calculators, and now covers everything under one name.

The tools are built with AI help (Claude) and tested before they go live: each calculator is checked against worked examples, and tax figures come from IRS publications cited on the page. The math engine behind the tax calculators is open source.

The free calculators are paid for by the templates and the business services. If something here helped you, that's how to keep it free.
